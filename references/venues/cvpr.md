Last verified: 2026-10-04. Venue ID: `cvpr`. Allowed metadata source: `openaccess.thecvf.com`.

Use the CVF Open Access menu at <https://openaccess.thecvf.com/menu> and include only entries whose visible menu link is labeled **Main Conference**. The menu separates Workshops, Findings, and Demos. The 2026-10-04 official listing union contained 602, 643, 783, 979, 1,294, 1,466, 1,660, 2,074, 2,353, 2,716, 2,871, and 4,042 unique paper URLs for 2015–2026 respectively (21,483 total). Editions 2015–2017 expose the list on their landing page; 2018–2020 require all three visible dated day pages; 2021–2026 expose an All Papers link. Include complete main-conference papers whether their presentation format was oral, spotlight, or poster; exclude workshop proceedings and poster-only abstracts without a full paper record.

CVF edition page structure varies by year. Follow the actual links visible on each edition page: use its **All Papers** link when present; otherwise collect every visible `Day N` page and union rows by the paper URL-derived native ID. Older `.py` pages may return an empty/error list for a guessed `?day=all`; do not synthesize pagination URLs. If there are no pagination links, use the paper rows on the edition page itself. Preserve each source page URL and observation time in the expected manifest. CVPR 2018 was verified to expose three dated day pages (979 unique rows across them) on 2026-10-04.

The official CVF menu and annual list pages enumerate papers but do not provide a verified bulk abstract/DOI export. Virtual-paper pages may reference JSON under `/static/virtual/data/`, but CVF's observed `robots.txt` disallows `/static`; do not request those endpoints. Open each official Open Access paper page for `citation_title`, ordered `citation_author`, the abstract block, publication date, and `citation_pdf_url`. Keep the visible official article URL and exact PDF URL; older CVF records may expose a legacy HTTP citation URL alongside an HTTPS PDF anchor on the official list row. Prefer that actually observed HTTPS list anchor and preserve any different citation value as run-local evidence. Read a DOI only if CVF detail metadata, visible links, or its official BibTeX contains one; otherwise record `not_present_on_official_page`. Do not infer DOI values from Crossref or construct PDF links from titles.

Do not normalize other name variants. Retain detail spelling for conflicting titles and names. If an official detail title begins exactly `Notice of Violation of IEEE Publication Principles:`, keep the expected identity and notification evidence in `metadata_exclusions.jsonl` as `non_research_content`; do not infer a retraction or exclude titles that merely mention violations elsewhere.
